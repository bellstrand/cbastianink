    The artist

    ✦

    I’m Sebastian, a tattoo artist based in Gothenburg, Sweden, where I’ve been living and working since late 2019. I’ve been tattooing full-time since 2014, specializing in Black & Grey, Realism, and Blackwork. I work on custom tattoos of all sizes, from smaller pieces to larger-scale projects.

    I’m open to working with a wide range of ideas and subjects, but I’m especially drawn to portraits of iconic musicians, artists, and pop culture characters from the ’60s through the ’90s, as well as dark art and Renaissance-inspired imagery.

    My approach is to create distinctive, thoughtfully designed tattoos that follow the natural shape of the body, with a strong focus on composition, contrast, and attention to detail.

    Sebastian
